Privacy Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Global Neighbors connects people who need help with neighbors, volunteers, and organizations. This policy explains what information we collect, who can see it, and the choices you have. We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name or organization name, whether you’re an individual or an organization, and the general location and region you enter. Your password is stored only as a salted, one-way hash; we can’t see it.
  • Profile details you choose to add: a profile photo or logo, a banner image, pronouns, a headline, a short bio or mission, languages, ways you can help, a website, and cause areas.
  • What you post: Help Board posts, replies, and private messages.
  • Safety records: reports you file, reports about you (including a copy of the reported content), members you block, and organization verification details such as legal name and registration number.
  • Technical information: a session cookie that keeps you logged in, and your IP address, which we use only to limit repeated sign-up, login, password-reset, and upload attempts. If you browse as a guest, your browser remembers that for the current tab only; we don’t create an account or store anything about you.

Photos

When you add a photo or banner, your browser resizes and re-saves it before uploading. This removes hidden metadata, such as the GPS location some phones attach to photos. When you remove or replace an image, the old one is deleted.

Who can see what

  • Help Board posts and individual profiles are visible only to signed-in members.
  • Organization profiles (name, logo, banner, tagline, location, mission, languages, what they offer, website, cause areas, verification status) are public.
  • Individuals’ profile photos and banners are visible only to signed-in members, like the rest of an individual’s profile.
  • Guests who browse without an account see sample posts and the organization directory, never members’ posts or individuals’ profiles.
  • A reply and its messages are visible only to the person who wrote the post and the person who replied.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members.
  • Moderators (Global Neighbors administrators) can see account details, reported content, and verification requests when needed to keep the community safe. Every moderator action is logged.
  • If you block someone, neither of you will see the other’s posts, replies, or messages.

Services that process data for us

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and database. All requests pass through Cloudflare’s network.
  • Google Fonts provides the site’s typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Resend (once email is switched on) delivers account emails such as confirmations and password resets.

These providers process information only to provide their services to us.

How long we keep information

  • We keep your account and content until you delete them.
  • Deleting your account (My profile → Delete account) permanently removes your profile, photos, posts, replies, messages, blocks, and verification requests, and signs you out everywhere.
  • Reports about an account, including the reported content, are kept after deletion as moderation records, so we can act on repeat abuse.
  • Records of sign-in and reset attempts, including IP addresses, are deleted within 7 days.
  • Sessions expire after 30 days, or when you log out.

Your choices

  • View and edit your profile at any time under My profile.
  • Delete individual posts, or your whole account, yourself.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask any privacy question, by emailing amoskow@brandeis.edu from the address on your account.

Keeping your information safe

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2, session tokens are stored only as hashes, and sign-in attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don’t use anywhere else, and never put your street address, financial details, or government ID numbers in a post.

Age requirement

Global Neighbors is for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please report it or email us, and we’ll remove it.

Changes

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we’ll update the date above and highlight the change in the app.

Contact
