Stop doing the same good work twice.

Global Neighbors shows nonprofits and NGOs who else is helping the same people in the same place, so they can split the work and waste less. Neighbors ask for help and offer it here too.

Need a hand, or have one to give? Start here.

Global Neighbors connects older adults, families, and anyone who needs help with neighbors, volunteers, nonprofits, and NGOs who can give it. Ask for something small or something big.

What you get

Older adults

Everyday help without feeling like a burden.

  • Ask for rides, groceries, heavy lifting, help with a phone, or company.
  • Message a helper and see their ratings. You can decline anyone.
  • Your email is never shown, and you share your address only when you’re ready.
Ask for a hand

People who need help

Ask once and let help come to you.

  • One post reaches neighbors, volunteers, and organizations.
  • Say what you need, when, and how many people it takes.
  • Conversations stay private between you and the person who replied.
Post what you need

People offering help

Spend your time where it’s still needed.

  • Find requests near you, by date, or by how urgent they are.
  • See how many helpers a task still needs before you commit.
  • Volunteer or find paid tasks, and build a rating people can trust.
Offer your help

Nonprofits and NGOs

Find partners first, then share the load.

  • Search organizations by cause and region to see who already serves your community.
  • Post one request for up to 20 helpers and track who has committed.
  • Earn a Verified badge once your registration is checked.

Our promise: less work done twice, more help delivered.

When two organizations serve the same people without knowing it, money, supplies, and volunteer hours get spent twice. We help nonprofits and NGOs find each other, so what they have goes further.

Good to know

What does it cost?
Joining, posting, and messaging are free.
Do I have to be in Boston?
No. We started in Greater Boston, and you can post from anywhere in the world.
How do I stay safe?
You can block or report anyone, and only members can see posts. Read our safety tips before you meet someone.
Is this for emergencies?
No. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number first (911 in the U.S.).
